## Description

Trae is ByteDance's AI-native IDE. It ships two surfaces: TraeCode, positioned as a '10x AI coding engineer' for developers, and TraeWork, a 'professional AI work assistant' for non-developer knowledge work, with a web edition at work.trae.ai. Framed around 'Ship Faster with Trae,' it integrates AI into coding and general work tasks in a single product line.
